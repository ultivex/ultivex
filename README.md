### Core Production Engines

#### 1. Structural Packaging & Converting
- **Mono-Cartons & Paperboard:** Folding Boxboard (FBB), Solid Bleached Sulfate (SBS), Kraft, and metallic met-pet cartons with high-precision die-cutting, embossing, and foil stamping.
- **Rigid Luxury Boxes:** Bespoke magnetic-closure boxes, book-style cases, and neck-and-shoulder rigid presentation boxes for high-ticket gifting, jewelry, and luxury confectioneries.
- **Heavy Industrial Corrugation:** 3-ply, 5-ply, and 7-ply heavy-duty shippers (A, B, C, and E flutes) engineered for export stacking strength, calibrated Burst Factor (BF), and Edge Crush Test (ECT) ratings.
- **Roll & Sheet Labels:** Self-adhesive roll labels, GHS-compliant chemical barrel labels, pharma-grade barcode labels, tamper-evident security seals, and metallic BOPP finishes.

#### 2. Protective Polymers & Engineered Foam
- **Precision CNC Routing:** Custom-contoured Expanded Polyethylene (EPE) foam trays for industrial tools, valves, and automotive sub-assemblies.
- **Cleanroom & Specialized Grades:** Non-dusting, cross-linked XLPE foam tailored for medical devices, cleanroom diagnostics, and aerospace instruments.
- **Luxury Presentation Cushioning:** High-density EVA foam finished with rich velvet-flocking for executive presentation cases and fragile consumer goods.
- **Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Foam:** Conductive and static-dissipative foam inserts designed for sensitive PCBs, electronics, and instrumentation.

#### 3. Commercial, Marketing & Technical Print
- **Corporate Literature & Brochures:** Premium multi-page corporate profiles, bi-fold/tri-fold marketing brochures, and sales decks featuring soft-touch thermal lamination, spot UV, and tactile textures.
- **Presentation Folders & Sales Kits:** Custom die-cut pocket folders with document retainers and business card slots for tenders, proposals, and client onboarding.
- **Technical & Machinery Catalogues:** Heavy-duty, lay-flat PUR-bound machinery operating manuals, parts books, and engineering specification guides built for shop-floor durability.
- **Institutional & Publishing Print:** High-volume annual university journals, academic publications, clinical case files, and corporate stationery suites.
- **Exhibition & Event Collateral:** High-impact trade show backdrops, standee banners, product flyers, and experiential display collateral.

#### 4. Corporate Brand Experience & Executive Gifting
- **Turnkey Induction Hampers:** Custom employee welcome kits combining branded premium stationery, technical drinkware, apparel, and onboarding literature.
- **Executive & VIP Gifting:** Tailored leatherette presentation folios, bespoke client appreciation sets, and festival gift packaging.
